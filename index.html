Planner menu

Responsibilities

The tasks your team looks after. Add them one by one, or use Import / Export to work with Excel.

Use Excel to add or update many responsibilities at once. Start from the template so the columns match.

Move many responsibilities to one department at once.

Your responsibilities

Grouped by department – click a department name to fold it. Click ▸ for the description, the folder icon to change the department, ✕ to delete. To move many at once, use Set department. To give responsibilities to people, use Assign levels.

Assign levels

This is the one place to give responsibilities to people. Pick responsibilities and employees, choose a level, then press Apply level.

Employees

Add people one by one, or use Import / Export to work with Excel. To give people responsibilities, use Assign levels.

Use Excel to add or update many employees at once. Start from the template or an export so the columns match. Existing people are updated (matched by ID No. or name); empty cells keep the current value.

Move many people to one department at once.

Your team

Grouped by department – click a department name to fold it. Managers carry a MGR badge and can have people reporting to them. To change a manager, tick Manager on the new person and untick it on the previous one, then adjust “Reports to”. Managers are not used for level assignment.
